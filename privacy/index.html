Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-25

한국어日本語简体中文
In short: Table Club collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your records stay on your Apple Vision Pro.

Who we are

Table Club is a collection of three table games for Apple Vision Pro, made by HANA JUNG, an individual developer. Questions about this policy: angnimatiz@gmail.com.

What we collect

Nothing. Table Club has no servers and no network code of its own, so nothing you do in the games is sent to us.

Eyes, hands and surfaces

You play by looking and pinching, and by throwing with your hand. visionOS turns these into gestures; the app receives only the result (for example, the speed of a released dart) and uses it in real time. Table Club also asks what kind of surface its window rests on, so a table can lie flat on your desk. None of this is stored or transmitted.

What stays on your device

  • Your scores, records, ladder progress, nickname and settings — in the app's own storage.
  • Whether you own the Full Game and when your free trial started, remembered so the games open without waiting for the App Store.

This data stays on your Apple Vision Pro. It is included in your device backups according to your own iCloud settings, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your best scores are submitted to Game Center leaderboards, where other players can see them under your Game Center name. Game Center is Apple's service; see Apple's Privacy Policy.

In-App Purchase and the free trial

The 1-day free trial and the Full Game are offered through the App Store. Apple processes any payment; Table Club never sees your payment details or Apple Account. The app only asks StoreKit whether you own the Full Game and when the trial began.

Children

Table Club does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

隐私政策

生效日期 2026-10-25

简而言之：Table Club 不收集任何数据。没有账户、分析、广告和追踪。记录只保存在你的 Apple Vision Pro 上。

开发者

Table Club 是个人开发者 HANA JUNG 为 Apple Vision Pro 制作的桌面游戏合集。 联系方式：angnimatiz@gmail.com

我们收集的信息

没有。Table Club 没有服务器，也没有自己的网络代码，你在游戏中的操作不会发送给开发者。

目光、双手与放置的表面

你通过注视并捏合、或用手投掷来游玩。visionOS 将其转换为手势，应用只接收结果（例如松手时飞镖的速度）并即时使用。 为了让桌子平放在你的桌面上，应用也会确认窗口所在表面的类型。这些都不会被保存或传输。

保留在设备上的内容

  • 分数、记录、天梯进度、昵称和设置 — 保存在应用自己的存储空间中。
  • 是否拥有完整版以及免费试用的开始时间 — 以便无需等待 App Store 即可打开游戏。

这些数据保留在你的 Apple Vision Pro 上，按你的 iCloud 设置包含在设备备份中，删除应用时一并删除。

Game Center

如果你已登录 Game Center，你的最高分会提交到 Game Center 排行榜，其他玩家可以通过你的 Game Center 名称看到。 Game Center 是 Apple 的服务 — 参见 Apple 隐私政策。

App 内购买与免费试用

1 天免费试用和完整版通过 App Store 提供。付款由 Apple 处理，Table Club 看不到你的付款信息或 Apple 账户。 应用只向 StoreKit 询问你是否拥有完整版以及试用的开始时间。

儿童

我们不收集任何人（包括儿童）的个人信息。

变更

如本政策有变更，新版本将连同新的生效日期发布在此页面。
